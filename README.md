This dashboard provides a detailed analytical overview of sales performance across different dimensions such as geography, customers, products, and shipping. It visualizes the distribution of sales across the United States using a map, highlighting regions with higher revenue contribution. The dashboard identifies top customers based on both total sales and order frequency, helping to understand key contributors to business revenue. It also analyzes sales by customer segments (Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office), showing that the Consumer segment generates the highest sales. Product category performance is evaluated in terms of both sales and profit, where Technology and Office Supplies contribute significantly compared to Furniture. Additionally, shipping analysis reveals that Standard Class is the most frequently used shipping mode, while Same Day delivery has the shortest delivery time. Key insights such as the highest-selling city (Yuma), top-performing state (Wyoming), most profitable product, and least profitable product are also highlighted. Overall, this dashboard effectively combines multiple visualizations to deliver actionable insights for decision-making in sales strategy, customer targeting, and operational efficiency.
